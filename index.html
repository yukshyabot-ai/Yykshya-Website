// questions

Frequently asked.

Everything students ask before trying it.

Is the free trial really free?
+
Yes. 7 days, every feature, no card needed. Just message Yukshya on WhatsApp and your trial begins. After 7 days, pay if you want to continue — or just stop.
What file types can I save?
+
PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, JPG, PNG. Up to 19MB per file. If you send something unsupported, the bot will let you know.
How does "make pdf" work?
+
Type "make pdf", then send your photos one at a time (up to 20). When you're done, send "done" and Yukshya combines them into a single PDF — automatically resized and compressed so the file stays small. Send "cancel" any time to stop.
Is my data safe?
+
Files are stored securely and only accessible by you through your WhatsApp number. We don't share, sell, or read your files. WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption protects messages in transit.
How does the AI work?
+
When you type "explain" or "ask", the AI reads YOUR specific file and generates responses based on what's actually in your notes — not random Google answers. Personalised to your study material.
How do I pay?
+
Type "pay" for an automatic Razorpay link (cards, UPI, netbanking). Or "pay upi" to pay our UPI ID directly and submit the transaction ID with "paid".
How does the referral system work?
+
Type "refer" to get your unique 6-character code. Share it — friends type "ref YOURCODE" in their own chat. When 20 friends use your code AND pay for the ₹79 plan, you automatically get 4 months free. Tracked for you, no claiming needed.
