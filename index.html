FAQ

Got questions? We have answers.

Everything you need to know about Yukshya

Is it really free to start?
+
Yes! You get 7 days of full access — every feature, no limits, no credit card needed. Just message Yukshya on WhatsApp and your trial starts instantly. If you love it, upgrade for as low as ₹50.
What file types are supported?
+
Yukshya supports PDF, DOCX, PPTX, Excel files, and images (JPG, PNG). Each file can be up to 19MB. If you send something unsupported, Yukshya will let you know and suggest alternatives.
Is my data safe and private?
+
Absolutely. Your files are stored securely and only accessible by you through your WhatsApp number. We don't share, sell, or access your files. WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption protects your messages. Files are also backed up to your email for extra safety.
What if WhatsApp is slow or down?
+
Yukshya automatically sends a backup of your files to your registered email, so even if WhatsApp has issues, your notes are safe. Once WhatsApp is back up, everything works as normal.
How does the AI work?
+
When you use the "explain" or "ask" commands, Yukshya's AI reads the content of YOUR specific file and generates explanations based on what's actually in your notes. It's not generic internet answers — it's personalized to your study material.
How do I make payment?
+
Simply type "pay" in your Yukshya chat and you'll receive a secure payment link. We accept UPI, cards, and net banking. After payment, your account is upgraded instantly — no waiting, no manual verification.
How does the referral system work?
+
Type "refer" in text box to get your unique referral code. Share it with 5 friends. When a friend signs up and makes a payment using your code, you automatically get 1 month added to your plan — completely free. There's no limit to how many friends you can refer!
